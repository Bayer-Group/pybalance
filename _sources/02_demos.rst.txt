Demos
^^^^^^^^^^^^
The demos are grouped into three sections, listed in the sidebar:

- **Core** -- `MatchingData` and balance calculators, the building blocks used everywhere else.
- **Matching** -- the matchers: propensity score, linear program, cardinality-constrained, genetic, and aggregate-target matching.
- **Weighting** -- the weighters: IPTW and MAIC.

New to the library? Start with :doc:`demos/core_01_matching_data` and work through Core and Matching before Weighting.
